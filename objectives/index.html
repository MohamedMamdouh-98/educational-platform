Cognitive Objectives

At the end of this program, the student teacher should be able to:

61 Objectives
  1. 1Define TPACK model.
  2. 2Determine the dimensions of TPACK model in the field of mathematics education.
  3. 3
    Distinguish between:
    • Content knowledge.
    • Pedagogical knowledge.
    • Technological knowledge.
  4. 4Explain the importance of TPACK model in improving the educational process.
  5. 5Deduce the mathematical content knowledge.
  6. 6Deduce the pedagogical knowledge in teaching and learning mathematics.
  7. 7Deduce the technological knowledge in teaching and learning mathematics.
  8. 8Analyze a mathematics teaching situation in light of TPACK model.
  9. 9Define the concept of mathematical content analysis.
  10. 10State the importance of content analysis in teaching mathematics.
  11. 11Define mathematical concepts.
  12. 12Distinguish between concept, term, and definition.
  13. 13Define mathematical generalization.
  14. 14Define mathematical skills.
  15. 15Distinguish between elements of mathematical content.
  16. 16Design a digital mind map illustrating the relationships among elements of mathematical content.
  17. 17Analyze the content of a mathematics lesson using artificial intelligence applications.
  18. 18Define the concept of digital behavioral objectives.
  19. 19Determine the components of a digital behavioral objective.
  20. 20Classify digital objectives according to Bloom's taxonomy levels.
  21. 21Formulate measurable digital behavioral objectives using appropriate digital tools.
  22. 22Evaluate the behavioral objectives generated by artificial intelligence applications.
  23. 23Define the concept of prerequisite knowledge in mathematics.
  24. 24Explain the importance of identifying prerequisite knowledge before teaching mathematics lessons.
  25. 25Define the concept of digital warm-up in teaching mathematics.
  26. 26Explain the importance of digital warm-up in teaching mathematics.
  27. 27Distinguish between different types of digital warm-up suitable for various teaching situations.
  28. 28Use digital tools such as Padlet, Mentimeter, and Google Forms to implement lesson warm-up.
  29. 29Design digital warm-up activities suitable for mathematics lessons.
  30. 30Define the concept of digital representation of mathematical content.
  31. 31Explain the importance of using digital tools in representing mathematical content.
  32. 32Identify different forms of digital representation of mathematical content.
  33. 33Select appropriate digital tools for representing various mathematical concepts.
  34. 34Represent mathematical content using different digital formats.
  35. 35Design mathematical content using digital tools.
  36. 36Define the concept of digital teaching strategies in teaching mathematics.
  37. 37Distinguish between Strategy, Method, and Style.
  38. 38Explain the importance of using digital teaching strategies in improving mathematics learning.
  39. 39Identify different digital teaching strategies used in mathematics teaching.
  40. 40Select the appropriate digital teaching strategy according to the lesson objectives and the nature of the content.
  41. 41Apply some digital teaching strategies using digital educational tools or platforms.
  42. 42Define the concepts of digital classroom management and digital time management.
  43. 43Explain the importance of managing classroom and time in digital learning environments.
  44. 44Identify appropriate digital tools used for classroom and time management.
  45. 45Describe the roles of the teacher and students in a digital classroom.
  46. 46Apply digital classroom management strategies using appropriate tools.
  47. 47Organize lesson time efficiently using digital tools.
  48. 48Design a lesson plan that includes effective time management.
  49. 49Manage interaction and discussions in a digital classroom efficiently.
  50. 50Define the concept of digital lesson closure in mathematics.
  51. 51Explain the importance of closing a lesson in a digital learning environment.
  52. 52Identify key characteristics of an effective digital closure.
  53. 53Distinguish between different digital strategies used for lesson closure.
  54. 54Select appropriate digital tools for closing a mathematics lesson.
  55. 55Design an effective digital closure activity for a mathematics lesson.
  56. 56Define the concept of digital evaluation in teaching mathematics.
  57. 57Differentiate between measurement, assessment, and evaluation.
  58. 58Differentiate between types of evaluation (diagnostic, formative, summative).
  59. 59Design evaluation activities using digital tools such as Google Forms, Mentimeter, and Quizizz.
  60. 60Apply digital evaluation strategies in mathematics lessons.
  61. 61Provide immediate feedback using digital tools.

Affective Objectives

  1. 1Appreciate the importance of formulating digital behavioral objectives in improving the quality of education.
  2. 2Appreciate the role of digital representation in simplifying mathematical concepts.
  3. 3Appreciate the importance of digital tools in motivating learners.
  4. 4Appreciate the importance of organization and discipline in digital classrooms.
  5. 5Adhere to digital classroom rules and ethical behavior.
  6. 6Take responsibility for managing time effectively during learning.
  7. 7Appreciate the importance of digital evaluation in improving students' learning.
  8. 8Demonstrate willingness to actively participate in digital teaching activities without hesitation.
  9. 9Demonstrate an interest in continuously developing their digital skills to keep pace with technological developments in mathematics education.
  10. 10Demonstrate willingness to explore and adopt innovative digital tools and technologies to enhance mathematics teaching.
  11. 11Demonstrate willingness to share and exchange digital experiences with peers to improve teaching performance.
  12. 12Demonstrate acceptance of feedback on their digital teaching performance and use it to improve their teaching practices.
  13. 13Appreciate the importance of employing digital technologies in teaching mathematics and achieving its learning objectives.
  14. 14Demonstrate a positive attitude toward using digital tools and applications in teaching mathematics.
  15. 15Appreciate the importance of integrating mathematical content knowledge, pedagogical knowledge, and technological knowledge when designing digital instruction.
  16. 16Demonstrate a commitment to selecting digital tools appropriate to the nature of mathematical content, lesson objectives, and students' needs.
  17. 17Demonstrate a positive attitude toward self-directed learning and utilize digital resources to improve teaching performance.
  18. 18Demonstrate confidence in employing digital technologies in planning, implementing, and evaluating mathematics lessons.
