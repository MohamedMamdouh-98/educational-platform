Learning Strategy

Digital Flipped Classroom Strategy

Overview

The Digital Flipped Classroom Strategy is a modern teaching strategy that integrates digital technology into the teaching and learning process by reorganizing when and how students engage with instructional content. In this strategy, students are introduced to the learning materials before the classroom session through educational videos, digital presentations, online readings, and other electronic learning resources. Classroom time is then devoted to interactive activities, problem-solving, collaborative learning, discussions, and feedback under the teacher’s guidance.
