Roles in Digital Collaborative Learning

Role of the Teacher

  • Planner Sets objectives and designs the activity.
  • Technology Selector Chooses the right digital tool.
  • Facilitator Monitors group interaction.
  • Guide Supports students when needed.
  • Evaluator Assesses both group and individual work.
  • Feedback Provider Gives constructive feedback.

Role of the Student

  • Active Participant Engages in the task.
  • Collaborator Works with group members.
  • Digital User Uses tools effectively.
  • Problem Solver Contributes ideas.
  • Presenter Explains group results.
  • Responsible Learner Is accountable for their role.
