Implementation Procedures

  1. The teacher identifies a mathematical objective that requires collaboration and discussion.
  2. The teacher divides learners into small groups and assigns each group a clearly defined task.
  3. The teacher distributes roles, such as group coordinator, problem analyst, solution recorder, and presenter.
  4. The teacher creates a shared digital workspace for each group.
  5. Groups analyze the problem, exchange solutions, and document their reasoning and results.
  6. Each group presents its work to the other learners.
  7. The teacher discusses the solutions, compares different approaches, and provides feedback.
  8. The teacher assesses both group performance and individual contributions using defined criteria.
