Practical Application

Flipped Classroom in Practice

Mathematical Topic

Differentiation Rules

Digital Tools

Google Classroom & Google Forms

Implementation Procedures

  1. The teacher records a short video explaining the basic differentiation rules or selects an appropriate existing video.
  2. The teacher uploads the video to the course platform or shares its link through Google Classroom.
  3. The teacher attaches preliminary questions using Google Forms, such as differentiating f(x) = x² + 3x.
  4. Learners watch the video and answer the questions before the online session.
  5. The teacher reviews their responses to identify errors and learning difficulties.
  6. During the session, the teacher discusses the errors and asks learners to solve more advanced problems.
Advanced Problem for the Session
f(x) = 3x² + 2x − 5
