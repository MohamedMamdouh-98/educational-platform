Practical Application

Example in Mathematics

Mathematical Topic

Differentiation Rules

Digital Tools

Google Classroom, selected educational websites, GeoGebra, and Google Forms.

Implementation Steps

  1. 01 Introduction

    The teacher presents an engaging question about differentiation using PowerPoint or Canva to introduce the topic and stimulate learners’ curiosity.

  2. 02 Task

    The teacher explains the learning task and divides learners into groups using Google Classroom, assigning each group a differentiation rule to investigate.

  3. 03 Digital Resources

    The teacher shares selected educational websites, explanatory videos, and interactive resources through Google Classroom to guide learners’ research.

  4. 04 Process

    Learners explore digital resources, analyze examples, and solve differentiation problems collaboratively using GeoGebra and Google Docs. For example:

    Example f(x) = 3x² + 2x − 5 Therefore, f′(x) = 6x + 2
  5. 05 Evaluation

    Learners complete an online quiz using Google Forms, while the teacher evaluates their mathematical solutions and group presentations using a digital rubric.

  6. 06 Conclusion

    Learners present their findings using Google Slides, and the teacher summarizes the differentiation rules and provides additional exercises through Google Classroom.
