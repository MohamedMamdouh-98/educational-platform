Learning Strategy

WebQuest Strategy

Overview

The WebQuest Strategy is an inquiry-based teaching strategy that uses the Internet to organize learning activities requiring learners to search for, analyze, and interpret information and apply it to complete specific tasks and solve problems. It relies on digital resources selected in advance by the teacher and encourages learners to collaborate, exchange ideas, and produce meaningful learning outcomes.
