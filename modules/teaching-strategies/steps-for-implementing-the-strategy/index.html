Steps for Implementing the Strategy

Implementation Steps

  1. 01 Project Introduction

    The teacher presents a real-world problem or a meaningful question using PowerPoint or Canva to engage learners and explain the project objectives.

  2. 02 Project Planning

    The teacher organizes learners into groups using Google Classroom and assigns roles, tasks, deadlines, and expected outcomes.

  3. 03 Research and Information Gathering

    Learners collect information from reliable educational websites and digital resources, recording their findings using Google Docs or Google Sheets.

  4. 04 Project Development

    Learners collaborate to create a digital product, such as presentation, interactive model, infographic, or mathematical report, using GeoGebra, Canva, or Google Slides.

  5. 05 Presentation and Discussion

    Learners present their projects using Google Slides or Google Meet. The teacher may use the Breakout Rooms feature in Google Meet to facilitate small-group discussions and peer feedback.

  6. 06 Evaluation and Reflection

    The teacher assesses the final product and learners’ collaboration using digital rubric and Google Forms, then provides feedback and encourages learners to reflect on their learning.
