Stages of Implementing the Flipped Classroom Strategy

01
Stage One

Before the Classroom Session

The teacher prepares instructional materials in the form of educational videos, digital presentations, or online resources and makes them available to students in advance. Students review the materials and complete preliminary questions or tasks to prepare for the classroom activities.

02
Stage Two

During the Classroom Session

The teacher organizes individual and collaborative activities, guides students in solving mathematical problems, applying concepts, and discussing their solutions, and addresses any difficulties encountered during their preparation.

03
Stage Three

After the Classroom Session

The teacher monitors students’ learning through online quizzes, digital assignments, and self-assessment activities. Feedback is provided to help students correct misconceptions and consolidate their understanding.
