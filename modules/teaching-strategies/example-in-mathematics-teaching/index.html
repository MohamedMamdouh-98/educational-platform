Practical Application

Example in Mathematics Teaching

Mathematical Topic

Designing a Digital Park Using Geometric Shapes

Digital Tools

GeoGebra, Google Classroom, Google Meet (Breakout Rooms), Canva, and Google Slides.

Implementation Steps

  1. 01 Project Introduction

    The teacher presents a real-world challenge: How can we design a park using different geometric shapes while calculating its area and perimeter? The project objectives are introduced using PowerPoint.

  2. 02 Project Planning

    The teacher divides learners into groups using the Breakout Rooms feature in Google Meet. Each group plans its park design, assigns roles, and identifies the geometric shapes to include.

  3. 03 Research and Information Gathering

    Learners investigate the formulas for the area and perimeter of rectangles, squares, and triangles using selected educational websites. They record the formulas and calculations in Google Docs.

  4. 04 Project Development

    Learners use GeoGebra to design a digital park containing different geometric shapes and calculate the area and perimeter of each section. They use Canva to create an infographic showing the design and mathematical calculations.

  5. 05 Presentation and Discussion

    Each group presents its digital park using Google Slides, explains its design choices, and discusses its calculations with classmates.

  6. 06 Evaluation and Reflection

    The teacher assesses the accuracy of calculations, creativity, collaboration, and quality of the digital product using a rubric and Google Forms. Learners reflect on what they learned and how mathematics can be applied to real-life design problems.
